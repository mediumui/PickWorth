Who we are

About PickWorth

PickWorth exists because most AI tool coverage is either a listicle written from the pricing page, or a launch-day post with no testing behind it. We use the tools properly first, then write about what actually happened.

what we hold to

Editorial standards

Hands-on first

Nothing gets recommended that hasn't been used for real work. If we haven't tested something properly, the review says exactly that.

Willing to say no

A lot of these tools aren't worth paying for at all. Saying so plainly matters more to us than having something to link.

Corrections in the open

Tools change fast and we get things wrong. When we do, we fix the review and note what changed rather than quietly editing it away.

money

Who pays for this

No affiliate links, no paid verdicts

This site does not use affiliate links, and it never takes payment from a vendor to influence a review, a ranking, or a verdict. The only way it is funded is advertising — we plan to run Google AdSense, but no ads are live yet.

If you believe we've got something wrong, tell us. We'd rather correct it than be right.
