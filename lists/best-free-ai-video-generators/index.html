Buyer's guide

The best free AI video generators in 2026

Free no longer means a watermarked toy you outgrow in a day. Five text-to-video tools now hand out recurring credits with no card, and one of them - Kling - gives you more usable footage every morning than some paid tiers. The trick is knowing which free door fits the clip you actually need.

The short version

Start free, and start with Kling. Its roughly 60-66 daily credits - recurring, no card, no expiry timer - hand you several short clips every day, which is more than Pika's 30, PixVerse's 60-once-plus-30-daily, Hailuo's 3-5-a-day, or Luma's ~30-a-month pool. Pika is the gentlest first step if you just want one stylized clip fast, PixVerse gives the most generous one-time starter, Luma is the only free door near 1080p, and Hailuo feels the cleanest but caps you hardest per day. The real rule: none of these free allowances is permanent, so build on the clip quality, not on the credit number - and only pay when a watermark, commercial right, or resolution actually blocks the work.

Yes. Kling, Pika, PixVerse, Hailuo, and Luma all let you generate without a credit card. The limits are daily or monthly credit caps and watermarked, lower-resolution output, not a paywall at sign-up.

Kling, by a wide margin: its daily refill covers several 5-10 second clips every single day. Hailuo is close on a per-day basis (3-5 videos/day) but the daily cap is lower; Luma is the leanest at about 30 generations a month.

Free, but not the same free

Every tool below is free to start with no credit card, but "free" means four different things here: a daily drip of credits (Kling, Hailuo), a one-time starter pack that tops up a little each day (PixVerse), a small monthly pool (Luma, Pika), and a resolution ceiling that sits well below the paid tiers. Asking which is "the best free" without naming the clip you want - a quick social short, a 1080p测试, a daily content habit - produces a ranking that fits nobody. The short version: Kling wins on raw free volume, Luma wins on free resolution, and the other three are easier or cleaner doors in.

Kling: most free footage, every morning

Kling's free plan is the one to beat. It refills on the order of 60-66 credits every day with no card and no 30-day cliff, which translates to several 5-10 second clips daily - more recurring free output than any rival here. Physics and on-screen text hold up better than most free tiers, so the clips are usable past a demo. The catch is the 720p watermark and the per-clip length cap; you pay only when you need those gone or longer shots.

Pika: the gentlest first clip

Pika hands out around 30 credits a day - enough for roughly three short clips - and its editor is the friendliest of the group, so a first-time user gets from prompt to a shareable effect fast. Resolution sits at 720p with a watermark on free, and the credit pool is the smallest recurring one here, but for one-off social shorts the path is the shortest.

PixVerse: the biggest one-time starter pack

PixVerse leads on the first impression: a roughly 60-credit welcome pack plus about 30 credits a day keeps a new account busy for weeks before any cap bites. Free output is 540p and watermarked, which is the lowest resolution in this list, and the daily drip is smaller than Kling's, but the upfront generosity makes it the easiest door to trial many styles at once. Paid lands near $8 a month on annual billing for 720p and no watermark.

Luma: the only free door near 1080p

Luma's free pool is the leanest by count - about 30 generations a month - but each one renders near 1080p, a step above the 540p-720p free ceiling elsewhere. That makes it the pick when resolution matters more than volume, especially for image-to-video where Luma's lighting realism is its signature. There is no native audio and the monthly cap is real, so heavy users will feel it first.

Hailuo: cleanest feel, hardest daily cap

Hailuo (MiniMax) gives either 3-5 videos a day or a ~200-credit welcome bundle plus daily bonuses, no card required, and the output feels the most polished of the free set. But the daily video cap is the tightest here, so it suits occasional, higher-quality clips more than a daily content habit. The Creator tier near $14 a month is the jump for volume and consistency.

The picks, by what you need

If you want the most free video every day, Kling. If you want one quick stylized clip with the least friction, Pika. If you want to try many styles at once on a big welcome pack, PixVerse. If resolution matters and volume does not, Luma. If you want the cleanest occasional clip and can live with a tight daily cap, Hailuo. In every case, stay free until a watermark, a commercial right, or a resolution actually blocks the work - then the paid tiers start around $8-14 a month.
