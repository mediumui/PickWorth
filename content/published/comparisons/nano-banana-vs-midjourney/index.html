Nano Banana vs Midjourney

Updated 4 November 2026 PickWorth Editorial
4.5Nano Banana score

Who wins

They are not replacements for each other. Nano Banana is the more practical tool: free to start, exact on text where Midjourney guesses, and able to edit your own photo by conversation. Midjourney is the better artist: it wins on mood, texture, and a signature style that no instruction-following model reliably replicates, and its reference and community tooling keep studios loyal. If the image has words, is a real photo you are touching up, or must cost nothing to try, Nano Banana is the faster path. If the image is judged on how it feels, Midjourney is still the one to beat. Our rating leans to Nano Banana for accessibility and text, but the honest answer is that plenty of people end up using both.

The short answer. For AI image generation, Nano Banana is the practical pick and Midjourney the artistic one: choose Nano Banana when the image has text, is a real photo you are editing, or needs to be free, and choose Midjourney when the image is judged on mood, style, and a signature look. They are complements, not rivals.

Nano Banana or Midjourney, which should I pick?
Pick Nano Banana when the image has words on it, is a photo you are editing, needs to be photorealistic, or must be free to start. Pick Midjourney when the image is judged on mood, artistic style, and a signature look. Most working designers run both rather than choose one.
Who makes the better-looking image?
For artistic mood, composition, and a finished look, Midjourney. For photorealism, accurate materials, and following a detailed instruction, Nano Banana. Side-by-side tests with DALL·E, Midjourney, and FLUX consistently rank Nano Banana's photorealism first for people, products, and scenes.
Which one is free?
Nano Banana: the Gemini app gives every Google account a daily allowance of free image generations, about 20 a day on Basic as of 2026, though the cap is dynamic. Midjourney: no free plan; the only free taste is a limited trial inside the niji mobile app, and the core product starts at $10 a month.
Which handles text in the image better?
Nano Banana, clearly. It renders product labels, menus, and posters with the words spelled right and placed where asked, including curved surfaces and small print. Midjourney improved text handling in V8 but still struggles past a few words.
Which is better for editing my own photo?
Nano Banana, by conversation: you upload a picture and tell it what to change, and it edits the last image in the chat. Midjourney edits through variations, panning, zoom, and an inpainting Edit Model, which is powerful but less natural for a casual edit.
Which is faster and easier to use?
Nano Banana returns an image in 1-3 seconds on the API and under 10 seconds in the app, in a plain chat with no syntax. Midjourney returns a grid of four images per prompt and rewards learning its web app and Discord controls.
Which is cheaper for serious use?
Close. Nano Banana is free to start, then Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra raise limits; Midjourney runs $10, $30, $60, and $120 a month. Both land in the $20-30 a month range for daily work, but only Nano Banana has a usable free tier.
Does Midjourney have an API?
No public API, which makes it impossible to embed in a workflow or product. Nano Banana is callable through the Gemini API and Google AI Studio, pay-as-you-go, which is a deciding factor for developers.
What is PickWorth's call?
Rate Nano Banana higher for accessibility, text, and photo edits, and Midjourney higher for pure aesthetics. They are complements: Nano Banana for the work image and the free start, Midjourney for the art.

Where Nano Banana wins

Text, logos, and layouts

This is the gap that matters for real work. Where Midjourney still hallucinates letters, Nano Banana renders product labels, menus, invitations, and posters with the words spelled right and placed where asked, including curved surfaces and small print. Independent hands-on tests put it at exact on every text string they tried. If your image has copy on it, this alone is worth more than a prettier default style.

Editing your own photo by talking

You upload a picture and tell it what to change, and it edits the last image in the thread, so a multi-step retouch happens in one place. That is a different job from Midjourney's generate-a-grid-then-vary workflow, and for a casual edit of your own photo it is the easier of the two.

Photorealism and world knowledge

Because it rides on Gemini's understanding of the real world, it handles materials, lighting, and spatial sense better than a model that only knows aesthetics. Side-by-side tests with DALL·E, Midjourney, and FLUX rank its photorealism first for people, products, and scenes.

Free to start and built for work

The Gemini app gives every Google account a daily allowance of free generations, and the API is pay-as-you-go rather than credit-gated. Combined with the public API, that makes it the default for anyone who needs to embed image generation in a product or just try it without a card.

Where Midjourney wins

Artistic style and mood

For texture, atmosphere, and a distinctive authored look, Midjourney leads. Nano Banana follows instructions; it does not impose a signature style. If the brief is 'make it feel like a film still,' that is Midjourney's job, not this one's, and the gap is widest on editorial and concept work.

Consistency tooling and a community

Midjourney's style-reference and character-reference systems, moodboards, and the public Explore page are why studios stay loyal. You can lock a visual identity into reusable codes and learn from other members' prompts, which Nano Banana's conversational, single-thread editing does not match.

The grid and the Explore page

Every Midjourney prompt returns four images to choose from, and the Explore page lets you judge the style before you pay. Nano Banana returns one image per request, which is faster but gives you less to pick from.

The cost question

For daily work, the two land close: Nano Banana is free to start and then Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra raise the daily count and add Nano Banana Pro redo actions, while Midjourney runs $10, $30, $60, and $120 a month. Midjourney's real value tier is $30 for unlimited Relax images; Nano Banana's is the free tier, which needs no commitment at all. The decisive difference is the floor: Nano Banana has one, Midjourney does not.
