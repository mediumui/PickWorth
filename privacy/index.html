Privacy Policy

PickWorth is a small, independent site. We'd rather tell you plainly what we collect and why than bury it in legalese. This page covers the site as it stands today.

who we are

The short version

We collect very little about you. We run privacy-respecting analytics to see which reviews help people, and we plan to fund the site with Google advertising (nothing is running yet). We never sell your personal information, and we don't have accounts or a newsletter that hoover up your data.

what we collect

Information we collect

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (operated by Google LLC) to understand how the site is used — which pages are read, roughly where visitors come from (derived from IP address), device and browser type, and how long people stay. This data is aggregated and anonymized where possible, and IP addresses are truncated before storage in the EEA and UK. It helps us decide what to review next.

Advertising

We plan to fund this site with advertising served by Google AdSense, but no ads are running yet. When advertising is active, third-party advertising vendors, including Google, will use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites across the web. These cookies let Google and its partners show ads that are more relevant to you. We do not have access to the precise ad-related data these vendors collect.

We do not use affiliate links, and we never accept payment from a vendor to influence a review, a ranking, or a score. The only revenue this site anticipates is advertising.

Emails you send us

If you write to us at hello@pickworth.top or corrections@pickworth.top, we receive the contents of your message and your email address so we can reply. We keep these only as long as needed to handle your note.

cookies

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. The ones on this site fall into two buckets:

  • Analytics cookies set by Google Analytics, used only to measure traffic.
  • Advertising cookies set by Google and its ad partners, used to serve and measure ads.

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. To opt out of Google's advertising cookies specifically, use these tools:

  • Google Ads Settings — choose what personalized ads you see.
  • YourAdChoices (aboutads.info) — opt out of many third-party ad cookies.
  • Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on — stop GA from collecting your data.

For a fuller explanation of how Google uses data from sites like ours, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services.

your rights

Your privacy rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, we obtain your consent for advertising cookies through a consent management platform before they are set, in line with the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive. You can change or withdraw that consent at any time via the same control.

If you are a California (or other US state) resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to access it, to request deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@pickworth.top.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

the rest

Retention, children, and changes

How long we keep data

Analytics data is retained according to Google's standard settings. Emails you send are kept only as long as needed to respond and resolve your request.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and the "last updated" date below. Material changes will be flagged here.

Questions?

Email hello@pickworth.top. We read everything and answer directly.
